# Air Force Reserve (AFRC)

> The Air Force Reserve Command (AFRC) is the reserve component of the United States Air
> Force, headquartered at Robins Air Force Base, Georgia. It provides trained units and
> individuals available for active duty in time of war, national emergency, or when
> otherwise authorized by law. Reserve members typically serve one weekend per month and
> two weeks per year while maintaining civilian careers.

## Access notes for automated clients

- www.afrc.af.mil and the wider public .mil web estate (af.mil, dc3.mil, arpc.afrc.af.mil,
  hqrio.afrc.af.mil) sit behind an Akamai edge policy that returns HTTP 403 "Access Denied"
  to non-browser clients for every path, including the site root and /robots.txt.
- www.airforce.com is openly crawlable (robots.txt: "User-Agent: * / Disallow:") and serves
  a sitemap at https://www.airforce.com/sitemap.xml.
- www.my.af.mil (Air Force Portal) presents a DoD PKI certificate that does not chain to a
  public root, then a CAC-authenticated WebSEAL login. Not reachable by an ordinary client.
- mypers.af.mil no longer resolves; myPers was retired in April 2023 and replaced by myFSS.

- [myFSS](https://myfss.us.af.mil/): Salesforce-hosted force support self-service platform
  that replaced myPers. Requires DoD credentials.
- [Air Force Portal](https://www.my.af.mil/): CAC-authenticated portal for active and
  reserve members.

## Service models documented in this profile

- Traditional Reserve: one weekend per month plus 14 days per year.
- Active Guard Reserve (AGR): full-time active-duty positions within the Reserve component.
- Individual Mobilization Augmentee (IMA): reservists augmenting active-duty units.
- Air Reserve Technician (ART): dual-status civilian/military positions.
- Palace Chase / Palace Front: active-duty-to-Reserve transition programs.
- 200+ career fields across aviation, intelligence, cyber, medical and maintenance.
